# Course adjustment (Sprint 4)

Championships are at the same venue (Kenilworth Park). Headline predictors are **within-meet z-scores** of finish times in the **prior** championship:

\[
z_{i} = (t_{i} - \bar t_{\text{meet}}) / s_{\text{meet}}
\]

Star = team’s fastest runner’s \(z\); depth = fifth-fastest’s \(z\). Lower is faster.

This is the simple adjustment from the protocol: it does not require linking course names across invitationals. 2017 is rumored slightly long; using z within that meet still ranks runners that day.

Same-race \(z\) is **not** used to predict that race’s team place (that would be nearly tautological with scoring).
