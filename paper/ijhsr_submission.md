# IJHSR submission notes

Venue: [International Journal of High School Research](https://ijhsr.terrajournals.org/submissions.html#sub-1-2)

**Journal to select:** IJHSR – Engineering and Computer Science (data science / applied statistics). If the editor prefers a social-science frame, they may transfer to Business and Social Sciences.

**Article type:** Research Article (original data). Use the [IJHSR research template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Uafwph1Xx1Innsw6BSmUC04AWTQNS7va/edit).

**Files**
- Manuscript: `paper/LastName_FirstName.docx` and `paper/LastName_FirstName.pdf` — rename to your real last and first name before upload.
- Figures (also paste them into the Terra app as 300 dpi PNG): `paper/ijhsr_png/Figure1.png` … `Figure4.png`
- Rebuild: `python -m src.build_ijhsr`

**Before submit**
1. Replace `[Author Name]`, school **street address**, and a **personal** email (not school email).
2. Add `(mentor)` line only if a mentor should be listed; otherwise keep mentor in Acknowledgements.
3. Paste the same abstract and the five keywords into the Terra Journals App.
4. Export a PDF named `LastName_FirstName.pdf` if the app asks for PDF.
5. Confirm every DOI/URL.
6. Page count must stay ≤ 15 (body 10 pt Arial, single spaced; figures/tables excluded from the count).

**Keywords (must match the app)**
1. Computer Science and Software Engineering
2. Data Science
3. Sports analytics
4. Cross-country running
5. Predictive modeling
